Performance Engine
Optimizing LCP, INP, and CLS metrics out of the box by serving raw, pre-rendered semantic HTML trees direct from edge servers.
Global Scalability
Distributing lightning-fast customer layouts across a distributed global CDN, entirely free from old-school relational database query bottlenecks.

Why does EDS prioritize lightweight vanilla JavaScript over heavy frameworks?

Frameworks force mobile browsers to download heavy processing scripts before showing a single pixel. Vanilla block decorators transform markup instantly on load, protecting device battery life and search engine visibility.

How does instrumentation protect our enterprise design standards?

By writing strict component JSON properties, we establish strict design boundaries. Content authors gain absolute visual editing freedoms on the canvas without ever risking breaking global styling rules.
